# Chihuahua

The Chihuahua is the world's smallest recognised dog breed, but don't let their size fool you — these tiny dogs have enormous personalities. Originally from Mexico, Chihuahuas are fiercely loyal, surprisingly alert, and make devoted companions.

## Temperament

Chihuahuas are known for their bold, confident nature. Despite their diminutive size, they often behave as though they're much larger dogs. They form exceptionally strong bonds with their primary owner and can be wary of strangers.

## Health Considerations

- Patellar luxation
- Heart conditions (patent ductus arteriosus)
- Dental overcrowding
- Hypoglycaemia (especially in puppies)
- Tracheal collapse
- Hydrocephalus

## Exercise Needs

Despite their small size, Chihuahuas benefit from daily walks of 20–30 minutes. They enjoy short bursts of activity but can tire quickly. Always monitor in cold weather — they lose body heat rapidly.

## Grooming

Both smooth-coat and long-coat varieties exist. Smooth coats need minimal grooming (weekly brush). Long coats require more regular brushing to prevent tangles, particularly around the ears and tail.
